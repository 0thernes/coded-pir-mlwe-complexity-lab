# Statement Fidelity Audit (`docs/statement-fidelity.md`)

This document maps each headline theorem in `paper/main.tex` to its Lean 4.35.0 statement in `lean/Challenge.lean` / `lean/Solution.lean`, and separates what the Lean statement contains from what rests on computation or on prose. It was corrected on 2026-11-26 to match an independent statement audit; known mismatches between the paper and the Lean statements are listed in [`ERRATA.md`](../ERRATA.md). Kernel agreement certifies the proofs, not that the Lean definitions model the objects their names refer to.
