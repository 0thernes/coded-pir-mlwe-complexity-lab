# Limitations, Epistemic Boundaries, and Real vs. Metaphor (`LIMITATIONS.md`)

This document states precisely what `coded-pir-mlwe-complexity-lab` (`v5.4.0`) **does not prove**, where its formal verification boundary stops, and how conceptual inspirations relate to actual implemented code.

## 1. Open Problems Not Solved

This repository resolves no grand published open problem in Lean (`0 / 1,900` retired) and refutes no published claim (see [ERRATA.md](ERRATA.md) for the 2026-11-26 statement audit and its `v5.1.0`–`v5.4.0` resolutions).

1. **$\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ and Explicit Circuit Lower Bounds (`CS-001`, `CS-010`):** Nothing here proves or disproves $\mathrm{P} = \mathrm{NP}$ or gives super-linear explicit circuit lower bounds. `T3a` (multilinear-extension uniqueness), `T3b` (the counting inequality of the natural-proofs argument, with no PRF or circuit defined), `T3f` (pigeonhole counting) and `T3k` (Schwartz–Zippel) are standard facts.
2. **Matrix Multiplication Exponent $\omega = 2$ (`CS-004`) and $\mathrm{VP} \neq \mathrm{VNP}$ (`CS-012`):** `T3e`, `T3g`, `T3h`, `T3i`, `T3k`, and `T3m` give characteristic-free tensor-rank bounds: $\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle 2,2,2\rangle) = 7$ over every field (a known result), $\mathrm{rank}_F(T_3) \ge 5$, $\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle m,m,m\rangle) \ge 2m^2 - m$ (`T3i`), $\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle m,2,p\rangle) \ge mp+3$, **Bläser's (FOCS 1999) $5m^2 - 6m \le 2\,\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle m,m,m\rangle)$ over every field including $\mathbb{F}_2$ (`T3m`, surpassing Brockett–Dobkin's $2m^2-1$ for all $m \ge 6$)**, and the tightened two-sided bound $5\cdot 4^k - 6\cdot 2^k \le 2\,\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle 2^k,2^k,2^k\rangle) \le 2\cdot 7^k$. Asymptotic $\omega$ and $\mathrm{VP}$ versus $\mathrm{VNP}$ remain open.
3. **$C(2,4,2,2) \in [3/5, 8/13]$ (`CS-053`):** `T1e` gives Boolean checks (monotonicity, submodularity on 330 orbit representatives, 99 listed equalities, singleton Ingleton instances) for a tabulated function `h_uncond` with value ratio $8/13$. Closing `ERRATA.md` `L02`, Theorem `T1k` (`LinearRankSoundness.lean`) proves inside Lean that `matRankModP` ($\mathbb{F}_2, \mathbb{F}_3$) and `gf2RankSparse` ($\mathbb{F}_2$) equal the true vector-space span dimension `HasSpanDim`. Together with the standard fact that linear rank functions are entropic (outside Lean), `T1i`–`T1k` certify that no information inequality on (a) any 4 single variables, (b) any 5 of the retrieval objects, or (c) the 6 retrieval objects `{W1, W2, A1..A4}` separates `h_uncond`. Inequalities in 5 or more variables involving individual storage symbols are not excluded. That the 99 equalities encode every constraint of the $(2,4,2,2)$ PIR problem is not proved against a formal model. Whether a converse lowers the bound to $3/5$, or a non-linear scheme exceeds $3/5$, is open.
4. **PIR Schemes, Rates and Capacities (`CS-053`):** Closing `ERRATA.md` `S01`/`A02` completely inside the Lean kernel, Theorem `T1k` (`PIRProtocol.lean`) formalizes the general 2-file, $N$-server, $K$-MDS linear flag-private PIR protocol over any `SimpleField F`, proving exact zero-error decoding for both $\theta \in \{0,1\}$ and an explicit two-sided colluder-transcript bijection $\Psi_I$; and Theorem **`T1l`** (`PIRF101Cert.lean`, `v5.4.0`) proves **unconditional in-kernel constructive existence** over `SimpleField (Fin 101)` of the explicit $(6,3,3)$ rate-$10/17 > 4/7$ scheme certifying `DecInv * M_des = I_60`, `Cancel * M_pure = M_mixed`, and all 20/20 invertible $\mathrm{GL}_{20}(\mathbb{F}_{101})$ flag isomorphisms with zero `native_decide` and zero external oracles (also cross-verified in Python and ISO C11 `f101-t3k3`). General $T \ge 4$ zero-error schemes, general $M \ge 3$ capacity when $E \ge KT$, and the Sun–Tao–Xu–Zhang maximum-redundancy design question remain open; general capacity converses beyond the certified witnesses remain paper claims.
5. **Exact $\Theta(C_7) \in [\sqrt{10}, \vartheta(C_7) \approx 3.317667]$ (`CS-CMP-012`):** `T3j` gives the integer form of $\Theta(C_5) = \sqrt5$ (Lovász, 1979) and minimum fitting-matrix rank 4 for $C_7$ over every field. `T3l` is conditional: for every self-complementary conference graph on $4k+1$ vertices, $\alpha(G^{\boxtimes t})^2 \le (4k+1)^t$ and $\alpha(G^{\boxtimes 2m}) \ge (4k+1)^m$, with the hypotheses proved in Lean only for $P_5 = C_5$, $P_{13}$ and $P_{17}$ (and checked numerically up to $P_{41}$). Conference graphs do not exist for every $k$. Capacity $\sqrt n$ for self-complementary vertex-transitive graphs is Lovász (1979). $\Theta(C_7)$ is open.
6. **Worst-Case Quantum Hardness of MLWE (`CS-026`, `QC-042`):** `T2a` records that a diagonal with $m$ entries $q$ has product $q^m$ (the $q$-ary MLWE basis determinant); it refutes no claim, and `arXiv:2604.15858` (Luo, Part I) contains no determinant-ideal argument (that step is in Part IV, `arXiv:2605.17412`). `T2b` and `T2f` are DFT-cancellation identities; the link of `T2b` to Step 9 of Chen (ePrint 2024/555) is the author's proposal, and that bug was found by Hongxun Wu and, independently, Thomas Vidick. `T2c`–`T2i` are integer inequalities and moment tables; Rényi values, Bai et al. (2018) overheads and exact coupled FIPS 203 DFRs (90-digit verified) are numerical. None of this bears on MLWE hardness, which remains an unproven assumption.

## 2. Formal Verification vs. Computational Boundaries

- **Lean scope:** Lean checks algebraic identities over arbitrary commutative rings and fields (`T1a`, `T1f`, `T1k`, `T1l`, `T2a`, `T2b`, `T2c`, `T2f`, `T3a`, `T3e`, `T3g`–`T3m`), integer and natural-number inequalities (`T1b`, `T1c`, `T1d`, `T1g`, `T1h`, `T2c`–`T2i`, `T3b`–`T3d`, `T3f`, `T3j`, `T3l`, `T3m`), sound linear-algebraic rank certificates (`T1i`, `T1j`, `T1k`), the universal linear flag-private PIR protocol (`T1k`), and unconditional in-kernel constructive existence of the $(6,3,3)$ $\mathbb{F}_{101}$ rate-$10/17$ scheme (`T1l`). Challenge propositions use definitions from `TriadFrontier` (`ERRATA.md` `L09`).
- **Previously discrepant items closed in `v5.1.0`–`v5.4.0`:** `mlkemCompress1` (`L01`), in-Lean `HasSpanDim` soundness for `matRankModP` & `gf2RankSparse` (`L02`), non-degenerate $\mathbb{P}^3$ & `T1i`/`T1k`/`T2e`–`T2i` witnesses (`L06`), universal refutations & 11th adversarial test (`L07` + `adv_blaser_refutes_rank_71_at_m6`), the in-kernel $\mathbb{F}_{101}$ $(6,3,3)$ 20/20 zero-error witness (`S01`/`A02` via `T1k`+`T1l`), exact coupled FIPS 203 DFRs (`S02`), and Bläser's $\frac{5}{2}m^2-3m$ superseding `A10` (`T3m`) are all fixed and logged in [ERRATA.md](ERRATA.md).
- **Numerical layer (Tier 2):** Rényi divergences, Bai et al. (2018) optimisation, Lovász $\vartheta(C_7)$, DFT spectra, QGTL curves, Bareiss exact commutator determinant checks ($m=2..12$), and cross-checks of the explicit $\mathbb{F}_{101}$ $(6,3,3)$ matrix witness are evaluated outside Lean with 60-digit `Decimal`, ISO C11 `double`, and Eshkol v1.3.6 dual-number AD (agreement below `2e-13`). Coupled FIPS 203 DFRs (`e^T r - s^T(e_1 + c_u) + e_2 + c_v`, assuming standard coordinate independence of polynomial-product terms as in FIPS 203) are cross-verified by a 90-digit Kronecker exact interval oracle (width $< 10^{-30}$ bits) matching NIST FIPS 203 Table 1 within `0.04` bits ($2^{-138.77}, 2^{-164.81}, 2^{-174.76}$).
- **Samplers:** `triad_dyadic256/512/1024a/1024b/16384a/16384b_sample_ct` use branchless comparison masks in C11. This repository does not claim FIPS 140-3 certification, constant-time behaviour verified at the binary level across architectures, or hardware side-channel auditing.

## 3. Real vs. Metaphor (Tsotchke Toolstack Alignment)

Several architectural ideas were inspired by Tsotchke's public open-source toolstack (`eshkol`, `quantum-geometric-tensor-learning`, `moonlab`, `rosette-wire`), so we explicitly separate **what is literally executed** from **what is a clean-room conceptual adaptation**:

| Component | Literal / Real | Metaphorical / Clean-Room Adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| **Eshkol v1.3.6 Dual-Number AD (`eshkol/*.esk`)** | Literal Scheme/Eshkol-syntax source files (`renyi_cbd_ad.esk`, `koszul_pir_ad.esk`, `qgtl_lwe_berry_ad.esk`) evaluated deterministically by our standalone Node.js dual-number evaluator (`eshkol/run_eshkol_ad.mjs`) and differentially tested against 60-digit Python to `< 2e-13` (including `Dyadic-256` $|dR_2/d\sigma| = 7.77 \times 10^{-9}$ and `Dyadic-512` $|dR_2/d\sigma| = 3.57 \times 10^{-6}$). | We do **not** vendor or link the native LLVM Eshkol compiler binary; our clean-room runner executes the exact forward-mode dual-number calculus on the `.esk` definitions so CI runs zero-dependency on both Linux and Windows. |
| **QGTL Shifted-LWE Berry Curvature, PSD & Variational Law (`T2d`–`T2e`, `T2g`)** | Computes covariance functionals on discrete error tables that the paper names, by analogy, the Quantum Geometric Tensor antisymmetric Berry curvature $\Omega_{\theta\sigma}$ (no quantum state or curvature is defined in Lean), Provost–Vallée PSD $4\det(g) \ge \Omega_{\theta\sigma}^2$, Metric–Kurtosis determinant ordering $3/8 < 15/32 < 1/2$ (`Dyadic-512` matching exact Gaussian $\det(g) = 1/2$), and Pearson $\chi^2$/Hermite-4 variational Rényi-2 bound (`T2d`, `T2e`, `T2g`, C11, Python, Eshkol). | Clean-room mathematical adaptation of QGTL geometry (`CM-048`) to discrete LWE error distributions, **not** a physical quantum hardware experiment or a quantum speedup over lattice sieving. |
| **Moonlab C11 Style (`native/`)** | Literally compiled zero-warning `-std=c11 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror` C11 code with 90/90 unit + 1,024 fuzz tests and ASan/UBSan. | Clean-room C11 implementation written from scratch for this paper; does not vendor Moonlab source files. |
| **Rosette-Wire Receipts (`results/rosette_receipt.json`)** | Real deterministic SHA-256 JSON provenance receipt generated by `scripts/make_manifest.py` binding Lean axiom logs, witness JSONs, PDFs, and test digests, plus dual live DigiCert RFC 3161 TSA (`data/MANIFEST.sha256.tsr`) and 3-calendar Bitcoin OpenTimestamps (`data/MANIFEST.sha256.ots`) receipts. | Conceptual receipt format inspired by Rosette-Wire provenance envelopes; not tied to an external proprietary service. |
