# Limitations, Epistemic Boundaries, and Real vs. Metaphor (`LIMITATIONS.md`)

This document states precisely what `coded-pir-mlwe-complexity-lab` (`v5.1.0`) **does not prove**, where its formal verification boundary stops, and how conceptual inspirations relate to actual implemented code.

## 1. Open Problems Not Solved

This repository resolves no published open problem in Lean and refutes no published claim (corrected 2026-11-26 after an independent statement audit; see [ERRATA.md](ERRATA.md)).

1. **$\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ and Explicit Circuit Lower Bounds (`CS-001`, `CS-010`):** Nothing here proves or disproves $\mathrm{P} = \mathrm{NP}$ or gives super-linear explicit circuit lower bounds. `T3a` (multilinear-extension uniqueness), `T3b` (the counting inequality of the natural-proofs argument, with no PRF or circuit defined), `T3f` (pigeonhole counting) and `T3k` (Schwartz–Zippel) are standard facts.
2. **Matrix Multiplication Exponent $\omega = 2$ (`CS-004`) and $\mathrm{VP} \neq \mathrm{VNP}$ (`CS-012`):** `T3e`, `T3g`, `T3h`, `T3i` and `T3k` give characteristic-free tensor-rank bounds: $\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle 2,2,2\rangle) = 7$ over every field (a known result), $\mathrm{rank}_F(T_3) \ge 5$, $\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle m,m,m\rangle) \ge 2m^2 - m$ (weaker than the $2m^2-1$ of Brockett and Dobkin, 1978, as reported by Shpilka, `arXiv:cs/0201001`), $\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle m,2,p\rangle) \ge mp+3$, and the two-sided bound $2\cdot 4^k - 2^k \le \mathrm{rank}_F(\langle 2^k,2^k,2^k\rangle) \le 7^k$. Asymptotic $\omega$ and $\mathrm{VP}$ versus $\mathrm{VNP}$ remain open.
3. **$C(2,4,2,2) \in [3/5, 8/13]$ (`CS-053`):** `T1e` gives Boolean checks (monotonicity, submodularity on 330 orbit representatives, 99 listed equalities, singleton Ingleton instances) for a tabulated function `h_uncond` with value ratio $8/13$. With the rank routines `matRankModP` and `gf2RankSparse` assumed correct (they have no Lean correctness proof) and the standard fact that linear rank functions are entropic (outside Lean), `T1i` and `T1j` show that no information inequality on (a) any 4 single variables, (b) any 5 of the retrieval objects, or (c) the 6 retrieval objects `{W1, W2, A1..A4}` separates `h_uncond`. Inequalities in 5 or more variables that involve individual storage symbols are not excluded. That the 99 equalities encode every constraint of the $(2,4,2,2)$ PIR problem is not proved against a formal model. Whether a converse lowers the bound to $3/5$, or a non-linear scheme exceeds $3/5$, is open.
4. **PIR Schemes, Rates and Capacities (`CS-053`):** For `T1b`, `T1c`, `T1d`, `T1g` and `T1h`, Lean proves identities and inequalities between closed-form expressions and checks tabulated matrices; no PIR scheme, privacy, decodability, rate or capacity is defined in Lean. Achievability of the general rate formula for $T \ge 3$ is a paper claim. The Sun–Zhang capacity (`arXiv:2610.37412`) that the formula exceeds applies to linear, full-support-rank schemes with $N-U-2B \ge KT$. At $T = 2$ the formula is the rate of Theorem IV.4 of Sun, Tao, Xu and Zhang (`arXiv:2603.10769`). The rate $10/17$ at $(6,3,3)$ rests on a numerical rank computation (interference dimension 42 over $\mathbb{F}_7$ and $\mathbb{F}_{101}$); privacy and zero-error decoding of that design are not verified. The Sun–Tao–Xu–Zhang maximum-redundancy design question, zero-error schemes for $T \ge 3$, and general $M \ge 3$ capacity when $E \ge KT$ remain open.
5. **Exact $\Theta(C_7) \in [\sqrt{10}, \vartheta(C_7) \approx 3.317667]$ (`CS-CMP-012`):** `T3j` gives the integer form of $\Theta(C_5) = \sqrt5$ (Lovász, 1979) and minimum fitting-matrix rank 4 for $C_7$ over every field. `T3l` is conditional: for every self-complementary conference graph on $4k+1$ vertices, $\alpha(G^{\boxtimes t})^2 \le (4k+1)^t$ and $\alpha(G^{\boxtimes 2m}) \ge (4k+1)^m$, with the hypotheses proved in Lean only for $P_5 = C_5$, $P_{13}$ and $P_{17}$ (and checked numerically up to $P_{41}$). Conference graphs do not exist for every $k$. Capacity $\sqrt n$ for self-complementary vertex-transitive graphs is Lovász (1979). $\Theta(C_7)$ is open.
6. **Worst-Case Quantum Hardness of MLWE (`CS-026`, `QC-042`):** `T2a` records that a diagonal with $m$ entries $q$ has product $q^m$ (the $q$-ary MLWE basis determinant); it refutes no claim, and `arXiv:2604.15858` (Luo, Part I) contains no determinant-ideal argument (that step is in Part IV, `arXiv:2605.17412`). `T2b` and `T2f` are DFT-cancellation identities; the link of `T2b` to Step 9 of Chen (ePrint 2024/555) is the author's proposal, and that bug was found by Hongxun Wu and, independently, Thomas Vidick. `T2c`–`T2i` are integer inequalities and moment tables; Rényi values, Bai et al. (2018) overheads and DFRs are numerical. None of this bears on MLWE hardness, which remains an unproven assumption.

## 2. Formal Verification vs. Computational Boundaries

- **Lean scope:** Lean checks algebraic identities over arbitrary commutative rings and fields (`T1a`, `T1f`, `T2a`, `T2b`, `T2c`, `T2f`, `T3a`, `T3e`, `T3g`–`T3l`), integer and natural-number inequalities (`T1b`, `T1c`, `T1d`, `T1g`, `T1h`, `T2c`–`T2i`, `T3b`–`T3d`, `T3f`, `T3j`, `T3l`), and finite certificates (`T1c`, `T1e`, `T1i`, `T1j`, `T3e`). Kernel agreement certifies these proofs; it does not certify that definitions such as `mlweBasisDiag`, `koszulDen` or `mlkemCompress1` model the objects their names refer to. The challenge propositions use definitions from the solution's own library.
- **Previously discrepant `Compress_1` fixed in `v5.1.0` (`L01`):** In `v5.0.0`, `mlkemCompress1` differed from FIPS 203 $\mathrm{Compress}_1$ at $x = 832$. In `v5.1.0`, `mlkemCompress1` implements literal FIPS 203 Eq. (4.7) $\lfloor(4(x \bmod 3329)+3329)/6658\rfloor \bmod 2$ and proves exact equivalence with $\lfloor(2(x \bmod 3329)+1664)/3329\rfloor \bmod 2$ for all $x \in \mathbb{Z}$. See [ERRATA.md](ERRATA.md).
- **Unverified procedures inside Lean certificates:** `matRankModP` and `gf2RankSparse` have no theorem relating them to the dimension of a span; `T1i` and `T1j` rely on them (`ERRATA.md` `L02`).
- **Numerical layer (Tier 2):** Rényi divergences, Bai et al. (2018) optimisation, Lovász $\vartheta(C_7)$, DFT spectra and QGTL curves are computed outside Lean with 60-digit Python `Decimal`, C11 `double` and Eshkol v1.3.6 dual-number AD (agreement below `2e-13`). Decryption-failure rates are computed in a float64 numpy convolution model, not exactly. That model gives about $2^{-176.6}$ for standard ML-KEM-1024, against $2^{-174.8}$ in FIPS 203 Table 1; the cause of this difference is not yet explained. FIPS 203 states no $2^{-174}$ pass/fail threshold, so DFR comparisons are reported only relative to the same model's standard-parameter value.
- **Non-vacuity and adversarial tests:** `TriadFrontier/NonVacuity.lean` gives spot-check witnesses for selected hypotheses (including characteristic-2 `SimpleField (Fin 2)` in `v5.1.0`). It does not inhabit every hypothesis bundle: there is no instance of the `T2e`/`T2g`/`T2h`/`T2i` inequality hypotheses, of `MPoly`, or of the `T1i` functional hypothesis, and the `T1f` witness is degenerate. Of the 9 tests in `AdversarialTests.lean`, two are closed arithmetic facts (`L07`), while the `Compress_1` boundary test was fixed in `v5.1.0` (`L01`) to test literal FIPS 203 Eq. (4.7) failures ($b=0, e=+833$ and $b=1, e=+832$).
- **Samplers:** `triad_dyadic256/512/1024a/1024b/16384a/16384b_sample_ct` use branchless comparison masks in C11. This repository does not claim FIPS 140-3 certification, constant-time behaviour verified at the binary level across architectures, or hardware side-channel auditing.

## 3. Real vs. Metaphor (Tsotchke Toolstack Alignment)

Several architectural ideas were inspired by Tsotchke's public open-source toolstack (`eshkol`, `quantum-geometric-tensor-learning`, `moonlab`, `rosette-wire`), so we explicitly separate **what is literally executed** from **what is a clean-room conceptual adaptation**:

| Component | Literal / Real | Metaphorical / Clean-Room Adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| **Eshkol v1.3.6 Dual-Number AD (`eshkol/*.esk`)** | Literal Scheme/Eshkol-syntax source files (`renyi_cbd_ad.esk`, `koszul_pir_ad.esk`, `qgtl_lwe_berry_ad.esk`) evaluated deterministically by our standalone Node.js dual-number evaluator (`eshkol/run_eshkol_ad.mjs`) and differentially tested against 60-digit Python to `< 2e-13` (including `Dyadic-256` $|dR_2/d\sigma| = 7.77 \times 10^{-9}$ and `Dyadic-512` $|dR_2/d\sigma| = 3.57 \times 10^{-6}$). | We do **not** vendor or link the native LLVM Eshkol compiler binary; our clean-room runner executes the exact forward-mode dual-number calculus on the `.esk` definitions so CI runs zero-dependency on both Linux and Windows. |
| **QGTL Shifted-LWE Berry Curvature, PSD & Variational Law (`T2d`–`T2e`, `T2g`)** | Computes covariance functionals on discrete error tables that the paper names, by analogy, the Quantum Geometric Tensor antisymmetric Berry curvature $\Omega_{\theta\sigma}$ (no quantum state or curvature is defined in Lean), Provost–Vallée PSD $4\det(g) \ge \Omega_{\theta\sigma}^2$, Metric–Kurtosis determinant ordering $3/8 < 15/32 < 1/2$ (`Dyadic-512` matching exact Gaussian $\det(g) = 1/2$), and Pearson $\chi^2$/Hermite-4 variational Rényi-2 bound (`T2d`, `T2e`, `T2g`, C11, Python, Eshkol). | Clean-room mathematical adaptation of QGTL geometry (`CM-048`) to discrete LWE error distributions, **not** a physical quantum hardware experiment or a quantum speedup over lattice sieving. |
| **Moonlab C11 Style (`native/`)** | Literally compiled zero-warning `-std=c11 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror` C11 code with 87/87 unit + 1,024 fuzz tests and ASan/UBSan. | Clean-room C11 implementation written from scratch for this paper; does not vendor Moonlab source files. |
| **Rosette-Wire Receipts (`results/rosette_receipt.json`)** | Real deterministic SHA-256 JSON provenance receipt generated by `scripts/make_manifest.py` binding Lean axiom logs, witness JSONs, PDFs, and test digests, plus dual live DigiCert RFC 3161 TSA (`data/MANIFEST.sha256.tsr`) and 3-calendar Bitcoin OpenTimestamps (`data/MANIFEST.sha256.ots`) receipts. | Conceptual receipt format inspired by Rosette-Wire provenance envelopes; not tied to an external proprietary service. |
