# Limitations, Epistemic Boundaries, and Real vs. Metaphor (`LIMITATIONS.md`)

This document states precisely what `coded-pir-mlwe-complexity-lab` (`v4.0.0`) **does not prove**, where its formal verification boundary stops, and how conceptual inspirations relate to actual implemented code.

## 1. Open Problems Explicitly NOT Solved

1. **$\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ and Explicit Circuit Lower Bounds (`CS-001`, `CS-010`):** Nothing in this repository proves or disproves $\mathrm{P} = \mathrm{NP}$ or establishes super-linear explicit circuit lower bounds. Theorems `T3a`, `T3b`, and `T3f` formalize well-defined algebraic and combinatorial barrier cores (multilinear-extension uniqueness underlying Algebrization, PRF indistinguishability blocking Natural Proofs, and non-constructive Shannon counting).
2. **Matrix Multiplication Exponent $\omega = 2$ (`CS-004`) and Valiant's $\mathrm{VP} \neq \mathrm{VNP}$ (`CS-012`):** Theorems `T3e`, `T3g`, `T3h`, and `T3i` establish exact characteristic-free tensor rank bounds ($\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle 2,2,2\rangle) = 7$ over every field including $\mathbb{F}_2$, $\mathrm{rank}_F(T_3) \ge 5$, $\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle m,m,m\rangle) \ge 2m^2 - m$ via Brockett–Dobkin shattering the Koszul ceiling $m^2+m$ for all $m \ge 3$, and $\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle m,2,p\rangle) \ge mp+3$). Determining asymptotic $\omega$ or separating $\mathrm{VP}$ from $\mathrm{VNP}$ remains completely open.
3. **Non-Linear $C(2,4,2,2) \in [3/5, 8/13]$ (`CS-053`):** Theorems `T1e`, `T1g`, `T1h`, and `T1i` prove that NO Shannon inequality, NO 4-variable linear information inequality whatsoever (all 510 four-marginals of `h_uncond` lie inside the $\mathbb{F}_2/\mathbb{F}_3$ linear-rank cone $I_4$), and NO 5-variable $\mathbb{F}_2$-linear subspace-rank inequality on the retrieval subsystem (all 6 five-object retrieval marginals are $\mathbb{F}_2$-linear) can tighten the upper bound below $8/13$, while `T1f` establishes the projective $\mathbb{P}^3$ obstruction for linear 2-subspaces. Whether a genuinely $\ge 6$-variable non-Shannon inequality lowers the bound to $3/5$, or a non-linear scheme exceeds $3/5$, remains completely open.
4. **General $M \ge 3$ Non-LFSR PIR & Arbitrary $T \ge 4$ Zero-Error Schemes (`CS-053`):** Theorems `T1g`–`T1h` break the Sun–Zhang $E \ge KT$ infeasibility wall symmetrically for all $M \ge 3$ via a 1-flag sum scheme and prove exact contraction $\rho_{\mathrm{KV}}^j < \rho_{\mathrm{LFSR}}^j$, while `T1a`–`T1d` settle $M=2$ linear and $E=K+1$ unrestricted capacities. General $M \ge 3$ non-LFSR capacity above Sun–Zhang when $E \ge KT$ and deterministic zero-error constructions for arbitrary $T \ge 4$ remain open.
5. **Exact Zero-Error Shannon Capacity $\Theta(C_7) \in [\sqrt{10}, \vartheta(C_7) \approx 3.317667]$ (`CS-CMP-012`):** Theorem `T3j` formally proves exact $\Theta(C_5) = \sqrt{5}$ with zero reals via a 3-term $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{5}]$ SOS factorization and settles the exact minimum Haemers rank of $C_7$ as $4$ over every field $F$ (with $\alpha(C_7^{\boxtimes 2m}) \ge 10^m$). Computing the exact asymptotic value $\Theta(C_7)$ remains completely open.
6. **Worst-Case Quantum Hardness of MLWE (`CS-026`, `QC-042`):** Refuting Luo (2026) (`T2a`), proving the $m$-twisted DFT selection rule (`T2b`, `T2f`), and collapsing Bai et al. (2018) reduction loss via `Dyadic-256`/`512`/`1024-A/B` (`T2e`, `T2g`, `T2h`) do not prove that MLWE is immune to all quantum algorithms. Lattice hardness remains an unproven cryptographic assumption.

## 2. Formal Verification vs. Computational Boundaries

- **Zero-Mathlib Lean 4.35.0 Scope:** Lean machine-checks exact algebraic identities over arbitrary `CommRing`/`SimpleField` structures (`T1a`, `T1f`, `T2a`, `T2b`, `T2c`, `T2f`, `T3a`, `T3e`, `T3g`, `T3h`, `T3i`, `T3j`), exact integer/natural-number/$\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{5}]$ inequalities (`T1b`, `T1c`, `T1d`, `T1g`, `T1h`, `T2c`, `T2d`, `T2e`, `T2g`, `T2h`, `T3b`, `T3c`, `T3d`, `T3f`, `T3j`), and finite computational certificates (`T1c` $\mathbb{F}_{11}$ 90-matrix inverse certificate, `T1e` 330-orbit polymatroid, `T1i` 510-marginal $I_4$ & 6 five-object $\mathbb{F}_2$ realizability certs, `T3e` pure-integer left-inverses $L_2, L_3$).
- **Transcendental & Floating-Point Layer (Tier 2):** Transcendental exponentials, logarithms, Bai et al. (2018) continuous $\alpha$-$\sigma$ optimization, exact discrete-convolution DFRs (`Dyadic-256`/`512`/`1024-A/B`), trigonometric Lovász $\vartheta(C_7)$, continuous DFT spectra, and QGTL curves are verified outside Lean using 60-digit Python `Decimal`, C11 `double`, and Eshkol v1.3.6 dual-number AD (`< 2e-13` agreement).
- **Concrete Non-Vacuity vs. Cryptographic Production Validation:** `TriadFrontier/NonVacuity.lean` inhabits every algebraic typeclass (`SimpleField (Fin 5)`, `SimpleField (Fin 11)`, `CommRing Int`) so no universal theorem is vacuously true. While `triad_dyadic256_sample_ct`, `triad_dyadic512_sample_ct`, `triad_dyadic1024a_sample_ct`, and `triad_dyadic1024b_sample_ct` use branchless constant-time comparison masks in C11 and satisfy exact FIPS 203 DFR targets, this research repository does **not** claim FIPS 140-3 certification, compiler-level constant-time binary verification across all architectures, or hardware side-channel auditing.

Several architectural ideas were inspired by Tsotchke's public open-source toolstack (`eshkol`, `quantum-geometric-tensor-learning`, `moonlab`, `rosette-wire`), so we explicitly separate **what is literally executed** from **what is a clean-room conceptual adaptation**:

|---|---|---|
| **Eshkol v1.3.6 Dual-Number AD (`eshkol/*.esk`)** | Literal Scheme/Eshkol-syntax source files (`renyi_cbd_ad.esk`, `koszul_pir_ad.esk`, `qgtl_lwe_berry_ad.esk`) evaluated deterministically by our standalone Node.js dual-number evaluator (`eshkol/run_eshkol_ad.mjs`) and differentially tested against 60-digit Python to `< 2e-13` (including `Dyadic-256` $|dR_2/d\sigma| = 7.77 \times 10^{-9}$ and `Dyadic-512` $|dR_2/d\sigma| = 3.57 \times 10^{-6}$). | We do **not** vendor or link the native LLVM Eshkol compiler binary; our clean-room runner executes the exact forward-mode dual-number calculus on the `.esk` definitions so CI runs zero-dependency on both Linux and Windows. |
