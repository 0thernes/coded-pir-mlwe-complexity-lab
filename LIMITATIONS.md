# Limitations, Epistemic Boundaries, and Real vs. Metaphor (`LIMITATIONS.md`)

This document states precisely what `coded-pir-mlwe-complexity-lab` (`v5.0.0`) **does not prove**, where its formal verification boundary stops, and how conceptual inspirations relate to actual implemented code.

## 1. Open Problems Explicitly NOT Solved

1. **$\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ and Explicit Circuit Lower Bounds (`CS-001`, `CS-010`):** Nothing in this repository proves or disproves $\mathrm{P} = \mathrm{NP}$ or establishes super-linear explicit circuit lower bounds. Theorems `T3a`, `T3b`, `T3f`, and `T3k` formalize well-defined algebraic and combinatorial barrier cores (multilinear-extension uniqueness underlying Algebrization, PRF indistinguishability blocking Natural Proofs, non-constructive Shannon counting, and Schwartz–Zippel PIT).
2. **Matrix Multiplication Exponent $\omega = 2$ (`CS-004`) and Valiant's $\mathrm{VP} \neq \mathrm{VNP}$ (`CS-012`):** Theorems `T3e`, `T3g`, `T3h`, `T3i`, and `T3k` establish exact characteristic-free tensor rank bounds ($\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle 2,2,2\rangle) = 7$ over every field including $\mathbb{F}_2$, $\mathrm{rank}_F(T_3) \ge 5$, $\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle m,m,m\rangle) \ge 2m^2 - m$, $\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle m,2,p\rangle) \ge mp+3$, and the two-sided Strassen–Brockett–Dobkin sandwich $2\cdot 4^k - 2^k \le \mathrm{rank}_F(\langle 2^k,2^k,2^k\rangle) \le 7^k$). Determining asymptotic $\omega$ or separating $\mathrm{VP}$ from $\mathrm{VNP}$ remains completely open.
3. **Non-Linear $C(2,4,2,2) \in [3/5, 8/13]$ (`CS-053`):** Theorems `T1e` and `T1g`–`T1j` prove that NO Shannon inequality, NO 4-variable linear information inequality whatsoever (all 510 four-marginals of `h_uncond` lie inside $I_4$), and NO 6-variable linear or non-linear entropic inequality on the retrieval subsystem `{W1, W2, A1..A4}` (all 64 subsets are realized by a single explicit $\mathbb{F}_2^{64}$ arrangement in `T1j`!) can tighten the upper bound below $8/13$. Whether a genuinely **$\ge 7$-variable storage-coupled** non-Shannon inequality lowers the bound to $3/5$, or a non-linear scheme exceeds $3/5$, remains completely open.
4. **General $M \ge 3$ Non-LFSR PIR & Arbitrary $T \ge 4$ Zero-Error Schemes (`CS-053`):** Theorems `T1g`–`T1h` break the Sun–Zhang $E \ge KT$ infeasibility wall symmetrically for all $M \ge 3$ via a 1-flag sum scheme and prove exact contraction $\rho_{\mathrm{KV}}^j < \rho_{\mathrm{LFSR}}^j$, while `T1a`–`T1d` settle $M=2$ linear and $E=K+1$ unrestricted capacities. General $M \ge 3$ non-LFSR capacity above Sun–Zhang when $E \ge KT$ and deterministic zero-error constructions for arbitrary $T \ge 4$ remain open.
5. **Exact Zero-Error Shannon Capacity $\Theta(C_7) \in [\sqrt{10}, \vartheta(C_7) \approx 3.317667]$ (`CS-CMP-012`):** Theorems `T3j` and `T3l` formally prove exact $\Theta(G) = \sqrt{4k+1}$ for EVERY self-complementary conference graph (instantiated for $P_5 = C_5, P_{13}, P_{17}$ in Lean and $P_5..P_{41}$ in C11/Python) with zero reals and settle the exact minimum Haemers rank of $C_7$ as $4$ over every field $F$. Computing the exact asymptotic value $\Theta(C_7)$ remains completely open.
6. **Worst-Case Quantum Hardness of MLWE (`CS-026`, `QC-042`):** Refuting Luo (2026) (`T2a`), proving the $m$-twisted DFT selection rule (`T2b`, `T2f`), formalizing Bai et al. (2018) DPI/tensorization/$R_\infty$, exposing the ML-KEM-1024 Gaussian DFR Paradox, and collapsing Bai loss via `Dyadic-256`/`512`/`1024`/`16384-A/B` (`T2e`, `T2g`–`T2i`) do not prove that MLWE is immune to all quantum algorithms. Lattice hardness remains an unproven cryptographic assumption.

## 2. Formal Verification vs. Computational Boundaries

- **Zero-Mathlib Lean 4.35.0 Scope:** Lean machine-checks exact algebraic identities over arbitrary `CommRing`/`SimpleField` structures (`T1a`, `T1f`, `T2a`, `T2b`, `T2c`, `T2f`, `T3a`, `T3e`, `T3g`–`T3l`), exact integer/natural-number/$\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{p}]$ inequalities (`T1b`, `T1c`, `T1d`, `T1g`, `T1h`, `T2c`–`T2i`, `T3b`–`T3d`, `T3f`, `T3j`, `T3l`), finite computational certificates (`T1c`, `T1e`, `T1i`, `T1j` 64-subset $\mathbb{F}_2^{64}$ `PivotTrie`, `T3e`), concrete `NonVacuity` inhabitants, and 7 negative boundary refutations (`AdversarialTests.lean`).
- **Transcendental & Floating-Point Layer (Tier 2):** Transcendental exponentials, logarithms, Bai et al. (2018) continuous $\alpha$-$\sigma$ optimization, exact zero-trimmed discrete-convolution DFRs (including the ML-KEM-1024 Gaussian DFR Paradox), trigonometric Lovász $\vartheta(C_7)$, continuous DFT spectra, and QGTL curves are verified outside Lean using 60-digit Python `Decimal`, C11 `double`, and Eshkol v1.3.6 dual-number AD (`< 2e-13` agreement).
- **Concrete Non-Vacuity vs. Cryptographic Production Validation:** `TriadFrontier/NonVacuity.lean` inhabits every algebraic typeclass (`SimpleField (Fin 5)`, `SimpleField (Fin 11)`, `CommRing Int`) so no universal theorem is vacuously true. While `triad_dyadic256/512/1024a/1024b/16384a/16384b_sample_ct` use branchless constant-time comparison masks in C11 and satisfy exact FIPS 203 DFR targets, this research repository does **not** claim FIPS 140-3 certification, compiler-level constant-time binary verification across all architectures, or hardware side-channel auditing.

Several architectural ideas were inspired by Tsotchke's public open-source toolstack (`eshkol`, `quantum-geometric-tensor-learning`, `moonlab`, `rosette-wire`), so we explicitly separate **what is literally executed** from **what is a clean-room conceptual adaptation**:

| **Eshkol v1.3.6 Dual-Number AD (`eshkol/*.esk`)** | Literal Scheme/Eshkol-syntax source files (`renyi_cbd_ad.esk`, `koszul_pir_ad.esk`, `qgtl_lwe_berry_ad.esk`) evaluated deterministically by our standalone Node.js dual-number evaluator (`eshkol/run_eshkol_ad.mjs`) and differentially tested against 60-digit Python to `< 2e-13` (including `Dyadic-256` $|dR_2/d\sigma| = 7.77 \times 10^{-9}$ and `Dyadic-512` $|dR_2/d\sigma| = 3.57 \times 10^{-6}$). | We do **not** vendor or link the native LLVM Eshkol compiler binary; our clean-room runner executes the exact forward-mode dual-number calculus on the `.esk` definitions so CI runs zero-dependency on both Linux and Windows. |
