# Limitations, Epistemic Boundaries, and Real vs. Metaphor (`LIMITATIONS.md`)

This document states precisely what `coded-pir-mlwe-complexity-lab` (`v3.0.0`) **does not prove**, where its formal verification boundary stops, and how conceptual inspirations relate to actual implemented code.

## 1. Open Problems Explicitly NOT Solved

1. **$\mathrm{P} \neq \mathrm{NP}$ and Explicit Circuit Lower Bounds (`CS-001`, `CS-010`):** Nothing in this repository proves or disproves $\mathrm{P} = \mathrm{NP}$ or establishes super-linear explicit circuit lower bounds. Theorems `T3a`, `T3b`, and `T3f` formalize well-defined algebraic and combinatorial barrier cores (multilinear-extension uniqueness underlying Algebrization, PRF indistinguishability blocking Natural Proofs, and non-constructive Shannon counting).
2. **Matrix Multiplication Exponent $\omega = 2$ (`CS-004`) and Valiant's $\mathrm{VP} \neq \mathrm{VNP}$ (`CS-012`):** Theorems `T3e`, `T3g`, and `T3h` establish exact characteristic-free tensor rank bounds ($\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle 2,2,2\rangle) = 7$ over every field including $\mathbb{F}_2$, $\mathrm{rank}_F(T_3) \ge 5$, and $\mathrm{rank}_F(\langle m,m,m\rangle) \ge m^2 + 2$ for every $m \ge 2$ via Koszul flattenings, plus the degree-$(m-1)$ ceiling $m^2+m$). Determining asymptotic $\omega$ or separating $\mathrm{VP}$ from $\mathrm{VNP}$ remains completely open.
3. **Non-Linear $C(2,4,2,2) \in [3/5, 8/13]$ (`CS-053`):** Theorems `T1e`, `T1g`, and `T1h` prove that no Shannon inequality, no 4-variable Ingleton inequality, no Zhang–Yeung (1998) inequality, and none of the infinitely many Matúš (2007) non-Shannon inequalities $M_s$ ($s \in \mathbb{N}$) on the 12-variable unconditioned system can tighten the upper bound below $8/13$, while Theorem `T1f` establishes the projective $\mathbb{P}^3$ obstruction for linear 2-subspaces over arbitrary fields. Whether a genuinely $5+$-variable non-Shannon inequality lowers the upper bound to $3/5$, or an asymmetric non-linear scheme exceeds $3/5$, remains completely open.
4. **General $M \ge 3$ Non-LFSR PIR & Arbitrary $T \ge 4$ Zero-Error Schemes (`CS-053`):** Theorems `T1g`–`T1h` break the Sun–Zhang $E \ge KT$ infeasibility wall symmetrically for all $M \ge 3$ via a 1-flag sum scheme and prove exact contraction $\rho_{\mathrm{KV}}^j < \rho_{\mathrm{LFSR}}^j$, while `T1a`–`T1d` settle $M=2$ linear and $E=K+1$ unrestricted capacities. General $M \ge 3$ non-LFSR capacity above Sun–Zhang when $E \ge KT$ and deterministic zero-error constructions for arbitrary $T \ge 4$ remain open.
5. **Worst-Case Quantum Hardness of MLWE (`CS-026`, `QC-042`):** Refuting Luo (2026) (`T2a`), proving the $m$-twisted DFT selection rule (`T2b`, `T2f`), and tightening discrete-Gaussian reductions via `Dyadic-256` and `Dyadic-512` (`T2e`, `T2g`) do not prove that MLWE is immune to all quantum algorithms. Lattice hardness remains an unproven cryptographic assumption.

## 2. Formal Verification vs. Computational Boundaries

- **Zero-Mathlib Lean 4.35.0 Scope:** Lean machine-checks exact algebraic identities over arbitrary `CommRing`/`SimpleField` structures (`T1a`, `T1f`, `T2a`, `T2b`, `T2c`, `T2f`, `T3a`, `T3e`, `T3g`, `T3h`), exact integer/natural-number inequalities (`T1b`, `T1c`, `T1d`, `T1g`, `T1h`, `T2c`, `T2d`, `T2e`, `T2g`, `T3b`, `T3c`, `T3d`, `T3f`), and finite computational certificates (`T1c` $\mathbb{F}_{11}$ 90-matrix inverse certificate, `T1e` 330-orbit scale-128 polymatroid certificate, `T3e` pure-integer $\{-1,0,+1\}$ left-inverses $L_2, L_3$).
- **Transcendental & Floating-Point Layer (Tier 2):** Transcendental exponentials, logarithms, and exact discrete-convolution decryption-failure-rate (DFR) probabilities (60-digit Rényi-2 optimization for $\mathrm{CBD}_2/\mathrm{CBD}_3$/`Dyadic-256`/`Dyadic-512`, exact FIPS 203 ML-KEM-512/768/1024 DFR convolutions, continuous DFT spectra, and QGTL curves) are verified outside Lean using 60-digit Python `Decimal`, C11 `double`, and Eshkol v1.3.6 dual-number automatic differentiation (`< 2e-13` agreement). Lean does not construct $\mathbb{R}$ or $\exp/\log$; instead it certifies the exact underlying discrete moments, Cauchy–Schwarz and Pearson $\chi^2$/Hermite-4 variational bounds, cleared-denominator rational inequalities, and symmetry/non-vanishing theorems.
- **Concrete Non-Vacuity vs. Cryptographic Production Validation:** `TriadFrontier/NonVacuity.lean` inhabits every algebraic typeclass (`SimpleField (Fin 5)`, `SimpleField (Fin 11)`, `CommRing Int`) so no universal theorem is vacuously true. While `triad_dyadic256_sample_ct` and `triad_dyadic512_sample_ct` use branchless constant-time comparison masks in C11 and satisfy exact FIPS 203 DFR targets, this research repository does **not** claim FIPS 140-3 certification, compiler-level constant-time binary verification across all architectures, or hardware side-channel auditing.

## 3. Real vs. Metaphor (Tsotchke Toolstack Alignment)

Several architectural ideas were inspired by Tsotchke's public open-source toolstack (`eshkol`, `quantum-geometric-tensor-learning`, `moonlab`, `rosette-wire`), so we explicitly separate **what is literally executed** from **what is a clean-room conceptual adaptation**:

| Component | Literal / Real | Metaphorical / Clean-Room Adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| **Eshkol v1.3.6 Dual-Number AD (`eshkol/*.esk`)** | Literal Scheme/Eshkol-syntax source files (`renyi_cbd_ad.esk`, `koszul_pir_ad.esk`, `qgtl_lwe_berry_ad.esk`) evaluated deterministically by our standalone Node.js dual-number evaluator (`eshkol/run_eshkol_ad.mjs`) and differentially tested against 60-digit Python to `< 2e-13` (including `Dyadic-256` $|dR_2/d\sigma| = 7.77 \times 10^{-9}$ and `Dyadic-512` $|dR_2/d\sigma| = 3.57 \times 10^{-6}$). | We do **not** vendor or link the native LLVM Eshkol compiler binary; our clean-room runner executes the exact forward-mode dual-number calculus on the `.esk` definitions so CI runs zero-dependency on both Linux and Windows. |
| **QGTL Shifted-LWE Berry Curvature, PSD & Variational Law (`T2d`–`T2e`, `T2g`)** | Literally computes the exact discrete Quantum Geometric Tensor antisymmetric Berry curvature $\Omega_{\theta\sigma}$, Provost–Vallée PSD $4\det(g) \ge \Omega_{\theta\sigma}^2$, Metric–Kurtosis determinant ordering $3/8 < 15/32 < 1/2$ (`Dyadic-512` matching exact Gaussian $\det(g) = 1/2$), and Pearson $\chi^2$/Hermite-4 variational Rényi-2 bound (`T2d`, `T2e`, `T2g`, C11, Python, Eshkol). | Clean-room mathematical adaptation of QGTL geometry (`CM-048`) to discrete LWE error distributions, **not** a physical quantum hardware experiment or a quantum speedup over lattice sieving. |
