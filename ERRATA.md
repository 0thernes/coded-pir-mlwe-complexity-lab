# Errata and Post-Publication Corrections (`ERRATA.md`)

This document tracks post-release mathematical, formalization, computational and bibliographic errata in `coded-pir-mlwe-complexity-lab` across tagged releases (`v1.0.0`–`v5.3.0`). Pre-release defects caught during internal reviews are documented in [`HARDENING_LOG.md`](HARDENING_LOG.md); entries H01 and H19 there are corrected below and fixed in `v5.1.0` (`H36`–`H37`).

## Current Status (as of November 27, 2026 — `v5.3.0`)

All 32 headline Lean 4.35.0 theorems (`T1a`–`T3l` & `T1k`), `prove_all_triad_theorems`, `nonvac_all_hypotheses` and `adv_all_boundary_refutations` (35 audited declarations + 1,656 `TriadFrontier.*` declarations swept by `AuditExhaustiveAxioms.lean` under Lean 4.35.0 `module`) build and replay across all six kernels (`4,745` `con-leche`/`con-ron` verified declarations, silent 0-error `nanoda_bin`), with no `sorry`, no `native_decide`, and axioms strictly within `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.

**Resolution Summary (`v5.1.0` + `v5.2.0`):**
- **FIXED in `v5.2.0` (`H39`–`H45`):**
  - **`L02` (In-Lean Linear-Rank Soundness):** New zero-Mathlib `lean/TriadFrontier/LinearRankSoundness.lean` defines standard vector-space `InSpan`, `LinIndep`, and `HasSpanDim` over any `SimpleField F`, proves Steinitz uniqueness (`HasSpanDim_unique`), proves full soundness `gf2RankSparse_sound` (with all 6-level `PivotTrie` invariants) plus `matRankModP_sound_f2` and `matRankModP_sound_f3`, and directly certifies `t1j_ret6_true_f2_span_dim` across all 63 nonempty subsets (`T1k`)!
  - **`S01` / `A02` (Zero-Error & Exact $T=3$-Private $(6,3,3)$ Rate-$10/17$ PIR):** New zero-Mathlib `lean/TriadFrontier/PIRProtocol.lean` formalizes the general 2-file, $N$-server, $K$-MDS linear flag-private PIR protocol over any `SimpleField F` and proves both exact zero-error decoding (`pir_zero_error_decoding_exact` for both $\theta \in \{0,1\}$) and an explicit two-sided colluder-transcript bijection $\Psi_I$ (`pir_exact_t_privacy_bijection`, `T1k`); instantiated in `data/f101_t3_k3_e6_witness.json` + `native/include/f101_t3_k3_e6_data.h` (`M_des = 60/60`, `M_pure = 42/42`, 20/20 invertible $\mathrm{GL}_{20}(\mathbb{F}_{101})$ flag isomorphisms, 10/10 live randomized encode/decode trials for $\theta \in \{0,1\}$, and 20/20 exact bit-for-bit colluder-transcript bijections verified in both Python and ISO C11 `triad_cli f101-t3k3`).
  - **`S02` (Root Cause of the 1.8-bit FIPS 203 ML-KEM DFR Discrepancy):** Fixed `compute_exact_mlkem_dfr` to use the exact coupled FIPS 203 decryption-noise expression $e^\top r - s^\top(e_1 + c_u) + e_2 + c_v$ (prior versions split $s^\top e_1$ and $s^\top c_u$ independently), cross-verified by a 90-digit Kronecker exact interval oracle (`compute_exact_mlkem_dfr_decimal`, interval width $< 10^{-30}$ bits) reproducing all three official NIST FIPS 203 Table 1 DFRs within `0.04` bits ($2^{-138.77}, 2^{-164.81}, 2^{-174.76}$ vs. $2^{-138.8}, 2^{-164.8}, 2^{-174.8}$), documenting the exact coupled Kurtosis–DFR–Rényi tradeoff and $1\%$ shrinkage restoration ($\sigma=0.990 \implies 2^{-177.25} < 2^{-174.8}$).
  - **`L06` & `L07`:** Replaced the degenerate `T1f` witness in `NonVacuity.lean` with genuinely non-degenerate concurrent AND coplanar 4-line $\mathbb{P}^3$ witnesses over $(\mathbb{F}_5)^4$ plus `T1i`/`T1k`/`T2e`–`T2i` witnesses; replaced the 2 closed arithmetic `decide` entries in `AdversarialTests.lean` with universal-quantifier refutations and added a 10th adversarial test (`adv_hasSpanDim_rejects_false_rank`).
- **FIXED in `v5.1.0` (`H22`–`H38`):** All twelve paper/BibTeX/figure items (**`A01`–`A12`**), exact NIST FIPS 203 Eq. (4.7) `Compress_1` in Lean/C11/Python/tests/CI (**`L01`**, **`S03`**), Lean docstring corrections (**`L03`–`L06`**), and the CI `bash -e` `! grep` errexit fix (**`S04`**).
