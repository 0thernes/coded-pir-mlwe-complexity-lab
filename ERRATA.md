# Errata and Post-Publication Corrections (`ERRATA.md`)

This document tracks post-release mathematical, formalization, computational and bibliographic errata in `coded-pir-mlwe-complexity-lab` across tagged releases (`v1.0.0`–`v5.1.0`). Pre-release defects caught during internal reviews are documented in [`HARDENING_LOG.md`](HARDENING_LOG.md); entries H01 and H19 there are corrected below and fixed in `v5.1.0` (`H36`–`H37`).

## Current Status (as of November 26, 2026 — `v5.1.0`)

All 31 headline Lean 4.35.0 theorems (`T1a`–`T3l`), `prove_all_triad_theorems`, `nonvac_all_hypotheses` and `adv_all_boundary_refutations` (34 audited declarations + 1,330 `TriadFrontier.*` declarations swept by `AuditExhaustiveAxioms.lean`) build and replay across all six kernels (`4,337` `con-leche`/`con-ron` verified declarations, silent 0-error `nanoda_bin`), with no `sorry`, no `native_decide`, and axioms strictly within `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.

**Resolution Summary in `v5.1.0`:**
- **FIXED in `v5.1.0`:** All twelve paper/BibTeX/figure items (**`A01`–`A12`**), exact NIST FIPS 203 Eq. (4.7) `Compress_1` in Lean/C11/Python/tests/CI (**`L01`**, **`S03`**), Lean docstring corrections (**`L03`–`L06`**), float64 DFR honest reporting (**`S02`**), and the CI `bash -e` `! grep` errexit fix (**`S04`**), plus 3-cycle defensive hardening (`H22`–`H38`).
- **Open / Documented Scope Items (deliberately preserved transparently):** **`L02`** (`matRankModP`/`gf2RankSparse` span-dimension proof inside Lean), **`L07`** (2 closed arithmetic facts in `AdversarialTests.lean`), **`L08`** (legacy theorem identifier names preserved for backward compatibility and documented in prose), **`L09`** (`Challenge.lean` imports `TriadFrontier`), and **`S01`** (`verify_t3_k3_e6_zero_error` checks interference rank 42 only; 3-collusion privacy and zero-error decoding for $(6,3,3)$ remain unverified).
