# Errata and Post-Publication Corrections (`ERRATA.md`)

This document tracks post-release mathematical, formalization, computational and bibliographic errata in `coded-pir-mlwe-complexity-lab` across tagged releases (`v1.0.0`–`v5.0.0`). Pre-release defects caught during internal reviews are documented in [`HARDENING_LOG.md`](HARDENING_LOG.md); entries H01 and H19 there are corrected below.

## Current Status (as of November 26, 2026)

All 31 headline Lean 4.35.0 theorems (`T1a`–`T3l`), `prove_all_triad_theorems`, `nonvac_all_hypotheses` and `adv_all_boundary_refutations` (34 audited declarations) build and replay across the six kernels, with no `sorry`, no `native_decide`, and axioms within `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`. No Lean proof is known to be wrong. The errata below concern what the repository, the paper and some Lean definitions claim; they were found by the independent statement audit of 2026-11-26.

## 2026-11-26 statement audit

Audit log: <https://github.com/0thernes/0thernes-research-archive/blob/main/docs/reviews/2026-11-26-statement-audit.md> (audited commit `674b5dac3a94825cb7791ac74b38ffd0fef23f1c`).
